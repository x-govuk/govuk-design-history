A design history is a place to record what your team designed, what you learned and why you made decisions. Find out more about [what a design history is](/introduction/) and explore examples in the [directory](/directory/).

## Choose a platform

You can publish a design history on a platform that works for your team. For example, you could use an existing blog or another publishing tool.

Many teams make use of our templates to write their design histories using simple Markdown files, published as static websites hosted on free services like GitHub Pages or Netlify.

Find out how to [use Markdown and the templates](/using-markdown-and-the-templates/).

## Share your design history

Publishing your design history openly helps others learn from your work and build on your ideas. Share it publicly where you can.

If you’re using one of the templates, see [publishing the site](/publishing-the-site/) for deployment instructions.
